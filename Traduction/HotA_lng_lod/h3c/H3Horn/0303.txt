===== Map name =====
Horn of the Abyss

===== Map description =====
Bidley, Jeremy and Tark must defeat Casmetra before she activates the Horn of the Abyss. If Jeremy, Bidley or Tark are defeated in battle, the scenario will be lost. The hero level cap is 24. Bidley, Jeremy and Tark will proceed to the last scenario.
