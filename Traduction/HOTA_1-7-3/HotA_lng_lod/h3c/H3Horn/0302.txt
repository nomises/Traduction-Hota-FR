===== Map name =====
Heart of Water

===== Map description =====
Bidley must capture the town of Coralgate and escape the Plane of Water. If Bidley is defeated in battle, the scenario will be lost. The hero level cap is 18. After victory, Bidley will proceed to the next scenario.

Bidley had no idea where he found himself. Was he dead? The last thing he remembered was getting sucked into some kind of a swirl. Now, he was in this odd place with the rest of his crew. 				It felt as if he was standing on the seabed. High above his head, he could see fish and huge turtles. Were they swimming… or flying? The only thing he knew for sure was that whatever surrounded him, it wasn't water. Otherwise, he wouldn't be able to breathe or move as freelyas he did. It almost felt as if the air had gotten thick and humid, like some liquid, but runny enough for him to breathe it. 				There was a faded portal behind him. Somewhere on the other side of this portal, there were Jeremy, Tark and that shady Casmetra with the Horn. The realization hit Bidley hard: all of this was set up by the witch so she could get a hold of the artifact! While held in captivity by Dargem, Bidley overheard the renegade mage's rambling. He found out that Casmetra had hired the spellweaver to find the Horn, but when he failed, she got him trapped in a cavern. For that reason, he wanted to avenge himself and take the ancient relic. Bidley wished he'd given it all thought much earlier.
