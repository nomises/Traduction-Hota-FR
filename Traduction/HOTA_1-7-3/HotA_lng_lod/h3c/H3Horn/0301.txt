===== Map name =====
Frontier

===== Map description =====
To find his brother, Jeremy must find the Grail and build the Lodestar. If Jeremy is defeated in battle, the scenario will be lost. Leveling is capped at 16. Jeremy will proceed to the next scenario after victory.
