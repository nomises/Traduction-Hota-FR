===== Map name =====
Paradise of the Mind

===== Map description =====
This world is lush and bountiful, but all who tread its ground suffer from vivid dreams that fester in the mind. As time passes, the line between waking and sleeping blurs. You must find a cure for this strange affliction, and destroy all who stand in your way.
