===== Map name =====
Ice Assault

===== Map description =====
For centuries, the inhabitants of the icy land have stayed within their lands, fighting off raids from their neighbors drawn by the crystals and other riches. In recent months, however, the weather has grown colder, and now our enemies' realms are suffering from frost. It's time to strike back.
