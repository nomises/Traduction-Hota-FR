===== Map name =====
Invasion

===== Map description =====
For many years, no one dared to invade the northern lands, inhabited only by snow elves and other tribes, and of little value. But the necromancers weren't interested in gold or gems...

The North is harsh and inhospitable. Anyone who has ever dared to set foot on our lands knows this.		For many years, hardly anyone so much as dared to set foot on our frozen soil. Only occasionally, the rulers of neighboring lands, irked with the attacks of yeti and frost giants on their pastures, would launch punitive expeditions here, but those were short-lived; they understood they would be unable to consolidate their hold, let alone capture all of the northern wastelands.		But recently, a messenger from a southern village on the other side of the Rannock river brought alarming news: a huge army of the undead had attacked one of the northern kingdoms, quickly subjugating it.		Before we could do anything, a southern village fell to the undead, and soon they crossed the river and captured another one to the east.		This must stop. If the undead manage to gain a foothold on this shore, dislodging them may prove impossible. We must retake the village and strike back.
