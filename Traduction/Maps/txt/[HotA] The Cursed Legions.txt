===== Map name =====
The Cursed Legions

===== Map description =====
It all started suddenly. The rocky and volcanic lands were taken by necromancers, and some of the caves are overflowing with the dead. It seems there is no hope: the necromancers came in force and their skeletons are everywhere. However, armies from the main forts are eager to burn all in their path

===== Timed events =====

Name:
Старт - Темница
Message:
The rocky lands have served as a good home for troglodytes and harpies. Although many of your kind still remain in the caves, you've reclaimed most of the land. However, as luck would have it, a couple of problems have arisen. The first one is the necromancers. Their bony faces suddenly appeared near several dungeons, frightening many with their disgusting looks. There also hasn't been any word from the caves in a while, so it seems something has settled in there too. Fortunately, the second problem is a little easier: the demons. They are merciless and want to take your lands, aye; but it seems they have the same problems with necromancers as you do. You should try to reclaim the demon lands after taking back most of your dungeons. And, of course, drive away the necromancers. For now, it's best to dive into your stash and gather the resources you've been saving for a rainy day.

Name:
Старт - Инферно
Message:
You've been eyeing your neighbors' holdings for a long time. Warlocks have occupied the rocky lands, and their servants are living their best lives, even though they're more accustomed to dwelling in caves. You sense they're about to claim your necromancer-ridden territories and caves. Although, it seems the dead have pounced on them too. You need to hurry with three things: reclaim your fiery fortresses, thwart the warlocks' plans, and crush any undead that stand in your way. Fortunately, you have a small supply of resources, so you'll have fewer problems as you start.
