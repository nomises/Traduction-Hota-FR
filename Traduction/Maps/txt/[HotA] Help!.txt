===== Map name =====
Help!

===== Map description =====
You're searching far-off lands for the Grail when your steward sends you an urgent letter. The treasury is empty, your neighbors are at war, and there are monsters everywhere! You need to get back and set things right.

===== Timed events =====

Name:
intro
Message:
All your life you'd dreamt of being the one to find the Grail, but a lord's duties left little time for dreams. Then one night a stranger in a dark alley told you that the Grail was buried in a secluded forest a month's ride away. 		He must'd weaved some spellwork into his words, because you left that same night. Your trusty old steward was to manage things in your absence.

Name:
intro - cont.
Message:
As you return to your land in deep trouble you feel as if a curse struck the moment you left. Your steward, half-buried in frantic missives and letters entailing cries for help, is glad to have you back.		Curiously, you learn that not only did your rival lords vanish on the same night as you did; their territories are no better off than yours right now.

Name:
intro - fin.
Message:
What if it's actually a curse? You never saw the stranger's face... Could the four of you have fallen for one man's nefarious scheme? If so, why did he lure you away and impose misfortune upon your lands?		Maybe in time you'll find answers to these questions. Until then you have your work cut out for you. Restoring the flow of resources should be your top priority.

Name:
letter
Message:
Nestled between angry letters and dire tax reports you spot a clear-white envelope adorned with the sigil of a golden hare. It's a letter from the seer Jamison, who lives near the lands of the Necromancers.		Hastily written, it says that he will visit soon with important news. Until then he advises you to beware of a man named Sandro and seek out the Obelisks strewn across your lands.

Name:
visit
Message:
The seer Jamison arrives with a heap of answers. That Necromancer Sandro is behind it all. He lured you away and sowed chaos as a distraction so he could freely scour the lands.		What for? As it turns out, the Grail was buried here all along! Solving the riddle of the Obelisks will reveal its precise spot. Maybe your old dream will come true after all.

Name:
visit - cont.
Message:
As he turns to leave the seer Jamison tells you that fairness compels him to inform your rivals too. That makes it a five-way race for the Grail, including Sandro who the seer suspects is still out there, searching.		You welcome the challenge. And who knows? Maybe somewhere along the road you may even run into Sandro and have your chance to get back at him.
