===== Map name =====
The Tree Killer

===== Map description =====
To win, Dargem must amass 300 units of wood in 3 months. The maximum level of heroes is 24. After winning, Dargem and Murdoch, along with the Pendant of Holiness and the Pendant of Life, will proceed to the next scenario.
